## Post text (English)

Warfare 1347. A medieval strategy game for PC that I am building in Unity with AI, without a programming background.

What is there so far:
a map of Eurasia with six states and 205 settlements, generated by Python from elevation data;
a faction choice screen with landscape paintings made in ChatGPT;
a hero drawn first as concept sheets and then built in 3D, with a character editor;
towns with a Bannerlord-style market;
77 settlement models assembled by code in Blender, each culture with its own architecture.

Latest update: towns with a river running through them got water gates and quays, big towns sit on cobblestones, and every settlement flies its state's banner.
