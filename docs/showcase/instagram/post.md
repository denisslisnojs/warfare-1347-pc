## Post text (English)

Warfare 1347. A medieval strategy game for PC that I am building in Unity with AI, without a programming background.

This update rebuilds every settlement on the world map.

Six states, 77 models of towns, castles and villages. All of them are assembled by code in Blender: a script recolours houses from a free asset pack for each culture, so one building turns into a log cabin, a Norse turf-roof house or a southern tiled house.

Towns with a river running through them now get water gates in their walls, stone quays along the water, and houses over the water are removed automatically. Big towns sit on cobblestones, villages on trodden grass.

Swipe for before and after.
