## Project name

Warfare 1347: medieval strategy game (Unity, PC)

## Description

Medieval strategy game for PC in Unity 6 (C#, URP), in the spirit of Mount & Blade: a 3D campaign map of Eurasia with six states and 205 towns, castles and villages. The player's party travels across the map by pathfinding that avoids water and mountains and crosses rivers by bridges, with a game clock and x1/x2/x4 time speed.

The world is built by Python scripts: terrain, rivers, biomes and roads are generated from geographic data, and all 77 settlement models are made in Blender through code, each state with its own architecture. Every state starts from a lore document; its settlements are placed on the map to match it and checked automatically for slopes, water and reachability on foot. Game rules live in a separate C# core covered by 39 automated tests, and all game data is stored in JSON. Custom URP shaders render the terrain, buildings, water and about 180,000 trees.

In development: world map and travel done, battles at prototype stage.
